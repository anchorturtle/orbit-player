Privacy

No accounts. No tracking. Your settings stay on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no forms, no cookies for tracking, and no analytics or ad scripts on this site.

What stays in your browser

The player and gallery remember a few settings (like HD audio and the room view) in your browser's localStorage, and whether you closed the Astor Academy card in sessionStorage. These never leave your device. Clear your browser's site data to remove them.

Where files load from

  • The site, fonts and scripts (including three.js) are self-hosted on this domain. Nothing loads from Google Fonts or other script CDNs.
  • Some music and video files stream from GitHub's media host (media.githubusercontent.com). Like any web host, GitHub sees the request needed to deliver the file.
  • The site is served through Cloudflare, which handles the connection like any web host.

Links out

Links to other sites, like Astor Academy, open those sites under their own privacy rules.
